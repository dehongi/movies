Video quality depends on your subscription plan:
  • Basic: HD (720p)
  • Standard: Full HD (1080p)
  • Premium: 4K Ultra HD + HDR
The actual quality may vary based on your internet connection speed. Our adaptive streaming technology automatically adjusts quality for the best viewing experience.

We offer three subscription tiers:
  • Basic ($8.99/month): Access to all content in HD quality, 1 device at a time
  • Standard ($12.99/month): Full HD quality, 2 devices simultaneously, downloads enabled
  • Premium ($16.99/month): 4K Ultra HD + HDR, 5 devices simultaneously, downloads enabled, premium content access
All plans come with a 7-day free trial for new users.

You can cancel your subscription at any time through your Account Settings. Go to "Profile" > "Subscription" > "Cancel Subscription." Your subscription will remain active until the end of your current billing cycle. We don't offer prorated refunds for partial months.

We accept major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. In some regions, we also support local payment methods. Gift cards and promotional codes can also be applied to your account.

MovieHub is available on a wide range of devices:
  • Web browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
  • Mobile devices (iOS and Android apps)
  • Smart TVs (Samsung, LG, Sony, etc.)
  • Streaming devices (Roku, Apple TV, Chromecast, Amazon Fire TV)
  • Gaming consoles (PlayStation, Xbox)
For the best experience, we recommend using the latest version of your device's operating system and our app.

We recommend the following internet speeds for optimal streaming:
  • SD quality: 3+ Mbps
  • HD quality (720p): 5+ Mbps
  • Full HD quality (1080p): 10+ Mbps
  • 4K Ultra HD quality: 25+ Mbps
You can check your current internet speed in your account settings under "Connection Test."

If you're experiencing buffering:
  1. Check your internet connection speed
  2. Try lowering the video quality in the player settings
  3. Close other applications or devices using your network
  4. Restart your router or modem
  5. Connect to a wired connection instead of Wi-Fi if possible
  6. Clear your browser cache or app data
  7. Try a different device
If problems persist, please contact our technical support team.
