The ultimate goal of this project is to help video creators upload videos to this websitge and have option to have private, unlisted and public videos here. 
Also the goal of this website is to help them to upload automatically to other major platforms for videos and podcasts.

This website is not free for use though and users pay a monthly fee based on their total used space.
All videos can be public and we don't sell adds on them but users can do it themselves.
Videos that are flagged as copyright won't be deleted but set to private until dispute resolved.
